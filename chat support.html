Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I book a machine?
    To book a machine, navigate to the "Rent Machine" page, browse through available machines, select the one you need, choose your rental dates, and complete the booking process by providing your payment information.
  • Can I cancel a booking?
    Yes, you can cancel a booking up to 24 hours before the scheduled rental time. Go to "My Bookings" in your account, select the booking you wish to cancel, and click on the "Cancel Booking" button.
  • How do I list my machine?
    To list your machine, click on the "List Machine" button in the navigation bar. Fill out the form with details about your machine, upload clear photos, set your rental price and availability, and submit your listing for approval.
  • What payment methods are accepted?
    We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All transactions are secured with industry-standard encryption.
